About Fran

Hi! I'm Francine Miller, the founder and owner of Fran's Flowers, here in San Francisco, California! Flowers have always fascinated me, even when I was just a little girl. I loved picking them from my family's garden and making them into beautiful arrangements. Today, I am lucky enough to have that childhood hobby as my job, by arranging flowers for the people of San Francisco! It is so great to add to a space in the form of perfectly placed flowers. Please check out the rest of the site and visit my shop! I would love to be a part of your next floral need!
